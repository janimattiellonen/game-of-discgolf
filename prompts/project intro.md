# Project intro

Game Of discgolf

An isometric "2.5D" game from a bird's perspective.

A disc golf course with 9-18 "holes", each containing a teepad, a fairway with obstacles and a basket. The aim is to throw a disc from the teepad itto the basket with as few throws as possible.

The gameplay and rules are more like a adventure and will not reflect a realistic disgolf experience. The fairways will be built using tiles with different kinds of obstacles such as water (ponds, river, lake etc),sand buncers, maybe even lava and monsters. Different power-ups can be found which may provided extra speed, aiming aid etc.

Throwing mechanism is based on timing: direction and power. First you choose the direction (a arrow going left to right and you have to stop it at a certain point) and power (similar concept, you need to stop the power bar at a specific point).

Wind strength and direction will affect the flight of the disc. So will disc type too.
